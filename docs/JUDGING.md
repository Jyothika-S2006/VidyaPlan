# Judging Criteria — Inter-Institute IBM Bob Hackathon

## Four equally weighted areas (max 20 points total, 5 each)

### 01 Completeness and Feasibility (5 pts)
- Feasible plan and clear scope
- Complete proof of concept
- Meaningful use of IBM technology

### 02 Creativity and Innovation (5 pts)
- Original and distinctive approach
- Meaningful use of AI
- Clear solution differentiation

### 03 Design and Usability (5 pts)
- Clear and simple user experience
- Easy for users to adopt
- Practical real-world application

### 04 Effectiveness and Efficiency (5 pts)
- Solves a relevant problem
- Measurable impact
- Scalable to more users or use cases
